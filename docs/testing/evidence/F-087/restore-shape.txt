POST /MediaDash/RecycleBin/Items/Restore
Body {"itemIds":[..]} -> 409 'BinPath is required.'
Body {"BinPath":"..."} -> 404 (accepted, then not found)

POST /MediaDash/RecycleBin/AdoptBatch
Body {"paths":[..]} -> 400 'Path is required.' (singular, no batch)

GET /MediaDash/RecycleBin/Items -> item shape: FileName, SizeBytes, RecycledAtUtc, AutoPurgesAtUtc, HistoryId, OriginalPath, Provenance, Reason, IssueType, ActionText, RestoreHint. NO Id field.
